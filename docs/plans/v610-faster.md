## UAK-201 · The kit shows whether it is getting faster, and a lesson reaches the next agent without being asked for

- **Type:** feature
- **Priority:** P0
- **Estimate:** 8 h
- **Depends on:** UAK-197
- **Paths:** kit/.uak/bin/metrics, kit/.uak/bin/lib/metrics.py, kit/.uak/bin/lib/memory.sh, kit/.uak/bin/lib/claim.sh, kit/.uak/bin/lib/review-merge.sh, kit/.uak/bin/lib/hints.sh, kit/.uak/bin/wt, kit/.uak/bin/lib/commands.tsv, kit/.uak/docs/CLI.md, kit/.uak/tests/test_metrics.py, kit/.uak/tests/test_lessons_loop.py, kit/.uak/tests/timings/test_metrics.txt, kit/.uak/tests/timings/test_lessons_loop.txt
- **Acceptance:**
  - `uak metrics --trend` prints, per week and for the last seven days against the seven before: tasks integrated, the share rejected at least once, review rounds per task, the median from claim to INTEGRATED, the share that needed a human at merge, and how many times each lesson was shown; each line says the number of tasks behind it, and a week with fewer than five tasks says so instead of a percentage. Why: on 2026-11-25 the human asked whether the work was getting faster with the lessons recorded; `uak metrics` gave totals over 158 tasks (36 % rejected at least once, 1.61 rounds per task) and the per-day count had to be read by eye (28 on the 21st, 8 on the 25th); nothing said whether a lesson changed anything
  - a reject carries its cause: `uak review --verdict reject` takes `--cause` from a short fixed list (failure path not handled, test does not test, timing, process left, protocol, wording false, security, other) and refuses a reject without one; `uak metrics --trend` shows the causes per week, so "the same cause again" is visible. The list is in one place and CLI.md prints it
  - a lesson reaches the agent that needs it without being asked for: `uak claim` and `wt new` print the lessons tied to the task's Paths (the three most recent per path, in 300 characters each, as `uak next` shows them), and the record of the claim says which lessons were shown; today lessons are shown only by `uak next`, which the ledger counts 9 calls of in 158 tasks
  - a reject offers its lesson: after a reject the engine prints the one command that records it (`uak learn "…" --path … --task ID`) with the task's Paths filled in; `--learn "TEXT"` on the same `review` command records it in one step. Nothing is recorded without the reviewer writing the sentence
  - the loop can be judged: for each lesson, `uak metrics --trend` says how many tasks saw it and how many of those were later rejected with the lesson's cause, next to the same number for tasks before the lesson existed; with fewer than five tasks on a side it says "too few to tell"
  - no foreground process is added to a session start, `claim` and `wt new` take no more than 300 ms longer with ten lessons on the task's paths, and a project with no lessons prints nothing
- **Tests:** trend_compares_two_weeks, a_week_with_few_tasks_says_so, reject_needs_a_cause, causes_are_counted_per_week, claim_shows_the_lessons_of_its_paths, reject_offers_its_lesson, lesson_effect_is_counted_or_too_few, claim_is_not_slower
- **Verify:** `python3 kit/.uak/tests/test_metrics.py && python3 kit/.uak/tests/test_lessons_loop.py`
- **Next step:** `kit/.uak/bin/metrics` and `kit/.uak/bin/lib/metrics.py` (the ledger it reads); `learn_mutate` and `recall_run` in kit/.uak/bin/lib/memory.sh; where `uak next` prints lessons in kit/.uak/bin/lib/status.sh (read it, do not edit it); the gate's library `review-merge.sh` is the human's file, so the merge goes to the human
