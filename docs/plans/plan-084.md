## UAK-084 · A creativity index: every variant has its own identity motif and hero composition, and the capture flags siblings that came out the same

- **Type:** feature
- **Priority:** P1
- **Estimate:** 3 h
- **Depends on:** UAK-083
- **Paths:** kit/.uak/bin/lib/design_round.py, kit/.uak/bin/lib/design_breed.py, kit/.uak/bin/lib/design_metrics.py, kit/.uak/bin/lib/design_creativity.py, kit/.uak/tests/test_design_creativity.py, kit/.uak/bin/lib/commands.tsv, kit/.uak/docs/CLI.md, kit/.uak/docs/WHY.md, kit/.uak/tests/test_design_breed.py, kit/.uak/tests/test_design_breed_notes.py, kit/.uak/tests/test_design_breed_rules.py, kit/.uak/tests/test_design_round.py
- **Acceptance:**
  - `Design-Creativity` in PROJECT.md (1-5, default 3), overridable with `design round ... --creativity N`, is the round's creativity index; the genome records it and TASK.md says what it asks for (1: refine the parent, 3: same family and a new identity, 5: a new direction)
  - every genome has a `composition` gene, the hero's geometry in measurable terms, drawn from at least 6 archetypes (e.g. poster: the headline is the image, centered, at least 40% of the fold; stage full-bleed with the copy in a bottom band; stage left with the copy right; one object centered as a monument with the copy under it; editorial grid led by one huge number; split copy left with the stage right). No two siblings share one while archetypes remain. In a bred round, at creativity 3 or higher, a child's composition differs from its parent's unless the human's note on the parent praises the layout
  - every genome has a `motif`, the page's one identity idea, taken from a family that does not depend on the domain (craft or pattern of the place, an everyday object as the hero, a typographic poster, a cartography, documentary photography, an illustrated character). Siblings get distinct families. TASK.md asks for ONE motif carried strongly and for few objects: "identity, not quantity". The builder writes it as `<!-- motif: ... -->` at the top of index.html, and `design shoot` copies it into the genome
  - in a bred round, a child whose parent got a note praising it ("linda", "bonita", "creativa", "identidad", "me encanta"…) inherits the parent's literal motif: TASK.md quotes it as read from the parent's index.html, and the parent's note about too many objects becomes "keep the motif, cut the objects"
  - after a capture, QA_JS records a composition signature at 1920 (headline rect, stage rect and focal point, normalized; ground luminance; dominant hue of the fold). `design shoot` compares the signatures of the round's captured siblings. Two variants that are too close get the warning `clone of v<j>: same hero composition (headline …, stage …)`, and the round gets a creativity score from 0 to 100 (mean pairwise distance), printed by `design shoot` and `design report`
  - Why (colonIA R2, 2026-11-14): the six variants bred from R1 all came out the same: headline left, hex map right. R1/v1 was the best ("ESTA SUPER LINDA Y CREATIVA") because of its papel picado, a strong identity whose only problem was too many objects. The child kept the genes but lost the motif, since no gene carried it. The human: "deberías añadir un índice de creatividad y volver a hacer esta ronda, el problema está en que ahora todos son iguales … tenía muy bonitas banderas encima, una identidad muy marcada y linda, donde el único problema era que había demasiados objetos encima"
