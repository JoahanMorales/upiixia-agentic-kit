# Autopsy · MLB analytics hackathon, the v5 week (2026-10-16 → 10-22)

The second half of the 10-day hackathon in the [first autopsy](mlb-2026-10.md), and the first week on **uak
v5.0.0**: the team upgraded on 10-16 and ran it to the deadline. Source: the private team repo's `claims` branch
(1,638 commits in all): the black box, `inbox/log.md`, `DECISIONS.md`, reviews, queue, task and agent records,
plus `main` (203 new commits). The data is confidential, so this page holds counts only: no names, data or results.
Every number was recounted from the raw files; the scripts read the files as data.

## What it validated
v5 carried the team to the deadline on Windows, macOS, Claude Code, Codex and Cursor: 49 more tasks integrated,
per-SHA reviews and rejects that caught real problems, contracts as one line to many tasks, 14 lessons with
`learn`, follow-ups and handovers used for real. The weak point was **delivery**: the kit addressed a review or a
request to one identity, and identities outlived their sessions.

## What went wrong, and what changed in v5.1

## Still open
- Presence is activity on claims: an open session that runs no uak command looks offline. Offers move on only
  when someone runs `status`/`next`, or when the opt-in `uak-tick` Action runs.
- Identity reuse is per machine; an identity on another laptop is never taken over silently.
- `npm ci` runs only for `package-lock.json`; pnpm, yarn and bun lockfiles are linked when identical, not installed.
- Not changed: the 84 token-publishing commits. Subagents were 1.3% of the tokens (22M of 1.7B): the Haiku scout tier was almost unused; the map lines should take part of its job, the rest stays a rule.
- Not reproduced: a Codex note that `lint` and `next` read different backlogs.
